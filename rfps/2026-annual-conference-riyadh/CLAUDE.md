## Status

## What's being procured
End-to-end event-management services for a one-day, ~800-attendee corporate
conference in Riyadh, Q4 2026 — venue, AV/production, registration, catering,
branding, staffing, and permits, from a single vendor.

## Key decisions (from the intake interview — see `intake.md`)
- **Scope (in / out):** in — venue, AV, registration, catering, branding,
  photo/video, staffing, permits. out — travel/hotels, speaker fees, agenda
  content, swag.
- **Scale / quantities:** 800 attendees; plenary (800) + 3 breakouts (~250).
- **Mandatory requirements:** SCEGA-accredited venue + licensed organizer;
  Civil Defense approval; step-free accessibility; plenary recording;
  bilingual AR/EN registration & signage; licensed catering.
- **Budget range & pricing model:** SAR 1.2M–1.6M excl. VAT; fixed-price per
  the §9 breakdown; 15% VAT shown separately.
- **Timeline & key dates:** issue 1 Sep 2026; questions 8 Sep; submissions
  29 Sep; event Q4 2026 (date confirmed on award).
- **Evaluation criteria & weights:** 70% technical / 30% price, with a 60%
  technical threshold. Weights sum to 100% (see §8).
- **Vendor eligibility:** CR + SCEGA license; ≥ 3 comparable Saudi events
  (≥ 500 pax) in 3 years; 3 references.
- **Submission format & deadline:** separate technical + commercial PDFs;
  29 Sep 2026, 14:00 AST.
- **Compliance / legal constraints:** vendor secures all permits; catering
  from municipally licensed kitchens; KSA governing law.
- **Open questions:** exact event date (venue-dependent); possible 4th
  breakout track; Extend's standard payment terms ([TODO] in §10).
