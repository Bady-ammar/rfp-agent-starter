## 1. Introduction & background

Extend Co. holds an annual conference bringing together its employees and
business partners. In 2026 the event will host approximately **800 attendees
over one day in Riyadh** (Q4 2026; exact date confirmed on award based on
venue availability). Extend seeks a single event-management vendor to plan
and deliver the event end-to-end, to a standard that reflects the company's
brand to staff and partners alike.

## 2. Objectives

1. A professionally produced one-day conference for ~800 attendees with no
   registration queue exceeding 20 minutes at peak arrival.
2. A morning plenary for all attendees plus three parallel afternoon breakout
   tracks, with reliable AV and a recorded plenary.
3. A safe, fully permitted, and accessible event for all attendees.
4. A networking lunch that clears within the scheduled break for all guests.

## 3. Scope of work

**In scope:** venue sourcing & booking; stage and AV production; registration
system and on-site desks; catering coordination; event branding & signage;
photography & videography; on-site staffing and overall event management; and
all regulatory permits (SCEGA, Civil Defense, municipal).

**Out of scope:** attendee travel and accommodation; speaker fees/honoraria;
the event agenda and content (provided by Extend); and promotional
giveaways/swag procurement.

respond to each by number, stating compliance and how it will be met.

## 6. Vendor eligibility & qualifications (pass/fail)

- Valid Saudi Commercial Registration (CR) and **SCEGA organizer license**.
- At least **3 comparable corporate events (≥ 500 attendees)** delivered in
  Saudi Arabia in the last 3 years.
- Three contactable client references for those events.
- Evidence of financial capacity to carry event costs to milestone payments.

## 7. Proposal requirements (what to submit)

Submit two separate documents:

1. **Technical proposal** — response to every requirement in §4 by number;
   proposed venue(s); AV/production approach; registration plan with
   throughput calculation; catering plan; staffing chart; safety &
   accessibility plan; sustainability approach; team CVs; references.
2. **Commercial proposal** — the completed pricing table in §9.

PDF format. Keep the technical proposal under 30 pages excluding CVs and
annexes.

## 8. Evaluation criteria

Quality-led, **70% technical / 30% price**. A proposal must score **≥ 60% on
technical** to have its price opened. Method: highest combined weighted score
wins.

| Criterion                                                                  | Weight   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------:|
| Technical approach & production quality (incl. AV, registration, catering) | 35%      |
| Relevant experience & references                                           | 20%      |
| Team, staffing & on-site management                                        | 10%      |
| Accessibility, safety & sustainability                                     | 5%       |
| Price                                                                      | 30%      |
| **Total**                                                                  | **100%** |
