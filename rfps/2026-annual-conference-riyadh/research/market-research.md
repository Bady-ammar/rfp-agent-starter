# Market research — corporate event management (Riyadh, 2026)

Research behind the requirements in `../RFP.md`. Regulatory facts are sourced;
capacity and quality benchmarks marked *(planning assumption)* are sensible
defaults for an 800-person, one-day corporate conference and should be
confirmed with the venue/organizer — they are not sourced facts.

## 1. Who regulates business events in Saudi Arabia

- **Saudi Conventions and Exhibitions General Authority (SCEGA)** — the body
  that regulates the MICE sector, licenses event organizers, and accredits
  venues. Established by Royal Decree in 2018. Requiring a SCEGA-licensed
  organizer and a SCEGA-accredited venue is a reasonable mandatory gate.
  [Source: https://www.scega.gov.sa/en/Pages/About.aspx]
- **General Directorate of Civil Defense (998)** — fire safety, emergency
  exits, and crowd-safety approvals for the venue and the event setup. A
  valid Civil Defense safety approval for the configured space is mandatory.
  [Source: https://998.gov.sa/English/safety/SafetyInstructionList/Pages/default.aspx]
- **Event licensing overview** — large events also need municipal venue
  approval and, for larger gatherings, MOI security clearance; the organizer
  is expected to handle permits.
  [Source: https://thesaudigate.com/event-organization-and-licensing-regulations-in-saudi-arabia-a-complete-guide/]

## 2. Standards to require

- **ISO 20121:2024 — Event sustainability management systems.** The
  international standard for managing the social, economic, and environmental
  impact of events (Plan-Do-Check-Act). Asking bidders whether they hold or
  work to ISO 20121 is a fair *Desirable* criterion, and a *Mandatory* one
  for sustainability-conscious organizations.
  [Source: https://www.iso.org/standard/86389.html]
- **Accessibility — Mowaamah.** Saudi accessibility/inclusion program
  (Ministry of Human Resources & Social Development), levels Participant /
  Bronze / Silver / Gold. Require step-free access, accessible restrooms, and
  reserved seating; Mowaamah awareness from the organizer is a plus.
  [Source: https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/819480]

## 3. Tax & commercial

- **VAT is 15%** (ZATCA), applied to most goods and services. Pricing must be
  quoted **excluding VAT**, with 15% VAT shown as a separate line.
  [Source: https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/VAT/Pages/default.aspx]

## 4. Venue capacity context

- Riyadh has large accredited venues suitable for conferences — e.g. the
  Riyadh Front Exhibition & Conference Center (~70,000 sqm of space) — so an
  800-person single-hall plenary plus breakouts is well within market supply.
  [Source: https://www.scega.gov.sa/en/Pages/About.aspx]

## 5. Planning benchmarks *(assumptions — confirm, not sourced)*

For an 800-attendee, one-day conference:

- **Plenary hall:** theater seating for 800 with ≥ 1.0 m² per seated person
  in the gross hall area; clear sightlines to a main stage.
- **Registration throughput:** size desks/scanners so the 80th-percentile
  attendee clears registration in ≤ 20 minutes at peak arrival (rule of
  thumb: ~1 lane per 150–200 attendees arriving in the peak hour).
- **Connectivity:** ≥ 50 Mbps sustained per 100 concurrent users on event
  Wi-Fi, separate SSID for production.
- **Catering:** 1 buffet line per ~100 guests to clear a meal break in ≤ 45
  minutes; menus must include clearly labelled vegetarian and allergen
  information. (Catering must come from municipally licensed kitchens.)
- **AV:** main-stage LED/projection sized for back-row legibility (screen
  height ≥ 1/6 of the distance to the furthest seat); wireless mics with
  redundancy; recording of the plenary in 1080p minimum.
- **Staffing:** ushers/crowd marshals at ~1 per 100 attendees, plus
  registration, stage management, and a named on-site event director.

These belong in the RFP as measurable requirements (with M/D marks and
acceptance criteria), with the assumptions confirmed against the chosen
venue's actual specifications.
