## 1. Introduction & background
Who the issuing organization is, why this RFP exists, and the problem or
opportunity the winning vendor will address. 1–3 short paragraphs.

## 2. Objectives
The outcomes this engagement must achieve, as a short list. Outcomes, not
activities (e.g. "an event that hosts 800 attendees with zero registration
queues over 20 minutes" — not "provide registration desks").

## 3. Scope of work
What is **in** scope and what is explicitly **out** of scope. Be precise;
the in/out boundary is where disputes happen.

- **In scope:** …
- **Out of scope:** …

## 4. Requirements / specifications
The detailed, numbered requirements. Mark each as **Mandatory (M)** or
**Desirable (D)**. Every requirement must be specific, measurable, and
testable. Group by theme where helpful.

| # | Requirement | M/D | Acceptance criteria |
|---|-------------|-----|---------------------|
| 4.1 | … | M | … |
| 4.2 | … | D | … |

## 5. Deliverables & timeline
Concrete deliverables with dates or milestones. A table or simple schedule.

| Milestone | Deliverable | Target date |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| … | … | … |

## 6. Vendor eligibility & qualifications
Minimum requirements to bid (registration/licensing, relevant experience,
references, certifications, financial standing). Keep these as pass/fail
gates, separate from the scored evaluation in §8.

## 7. Proposal requirements (what to submit)
Exactly what a compliant proposal must contain, and in what structure — e.g.
technical proposal, commercial proposal (pricing), team CVs, references,
required forms. State the format and page limits if any.

## 8. Evaluation criteria
How proposals will be scored, with **explicit weights that sum to 100%**.
Separate technical from commercial. State the method (e.g. quality–cost
ratio) and any minimum technical threshold.

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|-------:|
| Technical approach & methodology | …% |
| Relevant experience & references | …% |
| Team & qualifications | …% |
| Price | …% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

## 9. Pricing instructions
How pricing must be presented — currency (SAR), VAT treatment, the cost
breakdown required, whether it's fixed-price or otherwise, and what's
included/excluded. Provide a pricing table the vendor must complete.

## 10. Terms & conditions
Payment terms, contract duration, SLAs/penalties, confidentiality, IP
ownership, warranties, governing law, and any mandatory compliance.

## 11. Submission instructions
Where and how to submit, the deadline (date, time, timezone), required
format, and the process for clarification questions. State that late or
non-compliant submissions will be rejected.

## 12. Appendices
Any forms, drawings, data, current-state documents, or reference material —
or pointers to the files in this RFP's `references/` folder.
